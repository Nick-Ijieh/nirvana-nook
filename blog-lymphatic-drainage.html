5 Benefits of Lymphatic Drainage Massage

Massage 12 June 2026

Lymphatic drainage massage is one of our most requested treatments — and for good reason. This gentle, rhythmic technique works with your body's natural lymphatic system to encourage drainage and support overall wellbeing.

Unlike deep tissue work, lymphatic drainage uses light, sweeping strokes that follow the direction of your lymph nodes. It's a soothing experience that leaves many guests feeling noticeably lighter by the end of the session.

Here are five benefits our guests notice most: reduced fluid retention and puffiness, support for the body's natural detoxification process, relief after long flights or periods of inactivity, a calming effect on the nervous system, and softer, more even-looking skin.

If you're new to this treatment, we recommend starting with a 60-minute session and staying well hydrated afterward. Many guests choose to pair it with our Purifying Facial as part of the Quiet Mind Ritual package.
